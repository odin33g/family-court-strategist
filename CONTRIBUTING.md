# Contributing to Family Court Strategist

First — thank you. If you're here, you probably know what it's like. Every improvement to this toolkit is another parent who doesn't have to figure it out alone.

## How You Can Help

### Share Your Experience
The most valuable contributions come from parents who've been through the system. If you've found patterns, developed templates, or learned lessons that aren't captured here, share them.

### Jurisdiction Adaptations
This toolkit was built around Australian family law (Family Law Act 1975, Federal Circuit and Family Court). If you can adapt the legal framework, templates, or strategy guidance for your jurisdiction, that makes this useful to parents worldwide.

**Jurisdictions we'd love to see covered:**
- United Kingdom (Children Act 1989)
- United States (varies by state)
- Canada (Divorce Act / provincial legislation)
- New Zealand (Care of Children Act 2004)
- South Africa (Children's Act 38 of 2005)

### Template Improvements
If you've found that a template is missing a field, or there's a document type we haven't covered, submit it. The more comprehensive the templates, the less parents miss.

### Plugin Skills
If you can write Claude Cowork skills and have ideas for new capabilities — mediation preparation, financial analysis, child welfare assessment — open an issue and let's talk about it.

### Translation
If you can translate the Getting Started guide or key templates into another language, that extends the reach significantly.

## Guidelines

### Keep It Evidence-Based
Everything in this toolkit links back to sources. Don't add analysis frameworks or strategies that aren't grounded in real legal practice or real case experience.

### Protect Privacy
Never include real names, case numbers, or identifying details in contributions. Use anonymised examples only.

### No Legal Advice
This toolkit helps parents organise evidence and identify patterns. It does not provide legal advice. Make sure contributions maintain that boundary clearly.

### Attribution Standards Apply
If your contribution includes quotes or referenced statements, follow the attribution protocol in `_system/attribution-standards.md`. This isn't optional — accuracy is the foundation.

## How to Submit

1. Fork this repository
2. Create a branch for your contribution
3. Make your changes
4. Submit a pull request with a clear description of what you're adding and why

If you're not comfortable with GitHub, open an Issue describing your contribution and we'll work with you to get it in.

## Code of Conduct

This project exists to help parents and protect children. Contributions must:

- Focus on the child's best interests
- Remain gender-neutral (the toolkit says "fathers" in some marketing because that's where it started, but the methodology works for any parent)
- Not advocate for any action that could harm a child
- Not include content that violates court orders or legal obligations
- Respect that both parents matter to a child's wellbeing
