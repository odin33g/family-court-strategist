- **Trigger** — what happened first
- **Response** — what happened next
- **Gap** — how much time between them
- **Pattern** — is this part of a recurring sequence

## Key Questions

For each pattern, ask:
1. Could this be coincidence, or is the timing too precise?
2. Who benefited from the timing?
3. Is there evidence of coordination (messages, calls, advice)?
4. Would a reasonable person find this suspicious?
5. Can you prove awareness (i.e., the person KNEW about the trigger event)?
