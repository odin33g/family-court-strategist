# Cross-Document Analysis

> The contradiction engine. Use this to systematically identify where accounts conflict, stories change, and credibility breaks down.

---

## How to Use

For every major event or claim in your case, check:
1. Does Person A's version match Person B's version?
2. Does the same person's account change between documents?
3. Do professional findings match what the parties claim?
4. Are there impossible timelines or logical contradictions?
