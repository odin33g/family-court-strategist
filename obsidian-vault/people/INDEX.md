# People Profiles

> Detailed profiles for every person involved in your case. Use the [[_templates/person_profile|person profile template]].

---

## How to Use

Create one file per person. Include:
- Their role in the case
- Key statements they've made (with attribution)
- Any concerns, conflicts of interest, or credibility issues
- Actions taken or not taken
