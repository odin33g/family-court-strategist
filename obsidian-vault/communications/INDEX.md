## Key Patterns to Track

### Contact Attempts
Track every attempt you make to contact the child or the other parent:
- Date and method (call, text, email)
- What you said/asked
- Whether you got a response
- If refused, what reason was given

### Response Rates
Calculate: out of X messages/calls, how many got responses? What percentage?

### Tone and Language
Note any changes in how the other party communicates:
- Cooperative → hostile
- Engaged → silent
- Responsive → blocking

### Evidence of Coaching
Look for language in child's statements that sounds rehearsed or adult-directed.
